// What Is This

Commputer is a Layer 1 blockchain that builds a distributed supercomputer from regular people's idle desktop resources.

No datacenters. No mining farms. No e-waste. Your existing computer is enough.

Contribute a small slice of your machine's idle resources. Earn $COMME. The more people join, the more powerful everyone's share becomes.

Scale Hurts

One desktop at 100% is the ceiling. Warehouses get punished. The protocol rewards regular people, not corporations.

Burns Everything

2 billion $COMME. Fixed supply. Dual burn mechanism. Every transaction fee, every compute job, every milestone — burned. Supply only goes down.

Patience Rewards

Your product gets better with age. As the network grows, your share of the communal computer grows with it. The wine of technology.

For Everyone

No VCs. No influencer deals. Anonymous founder. Word of mouth only. The product is the marketing.

// Get Started

01

Download

Get the node binary for your platform. One file, no dependencies.

curl -sSf https://commputer.xyz/install.sh | sh
02

Create Wallet

Generate your wallet. Write down the seed phrase. This is your identity.

commputer wallet create
03

Start Contributing

Run the node. Choose how much of your computer to contribute. Start earning.

commputer run --contribution 50
04

Earn $COMME

Your idle resources contribute to the communal supercomputer. You earn $COMME for every proof you complete. Check your balance anytime.

commputer wallet show

FAQ

What does it cost?

Nothing. You contribute idle resources your computer isn't using. Electricity cost is typically under $5/month.

How much will I earn?

Depends on network size and your hardware. At launch, a single desktop earns approximately 0.09 $COMME per day. As the network grows, the rate adjusts — but so does the value of what you earn.

Is it safe?

Open source. Verify yourself. The node runs in user space with no elevated permissions. It only uses the resources you allocate.

What happens if I turn off my computer?

You stop earning while offline. Your balance and wallet are safe. The network has a grace period system — long-time contributors have built-up tolerance for downtime.

Who built this?

An anonymous founder. No company. No investors. The code is the product. The community is the team.
