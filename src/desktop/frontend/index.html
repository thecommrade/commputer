Welcome to Commputer

A communal supercomputer for the people, by the people.

This dashboard connects to your running Commputer node and shows:

  • Your wallet balance and tier progress
  • Mining status and proof scores
  • Network peers and compliance
  • Transaction history

Your Wallet

The Wallet panel on the left shows your address, balance, and tier.

Your tier determines what communal resources you can access:

  • Base (1+ COMME): Analytics access
  • Storage (10+ COMME): Communal storage
  • Compute (20+ COMME): Communal compute
  • Full (33+ COMME): Full personal computer + AI

Mining & Contributions

The Contribution slider controls how much of your resources to share.

The Mining Status panel shows your proof scores across 5 channels:

CPU, GPU, Storage, RAM, and Bandwidth.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Use these shortcuts for quick access:

  • Ctrl+S — Open Send dialog
  • Ctrl+W — Focus Wallet panel
  • Ctrl+M — Focus Mining panel
  • Ctrl+L — Toggle Log viewer
  • Escape — Close dialogs
